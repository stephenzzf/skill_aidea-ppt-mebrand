# Official Presentations Method Integration

This skill uses the official Codex `Presentations` skill as a method reference, not as the default runtime.

## Adopted Workflow

1. Source/reference audit: identify source content, visual references, identity assets, and missing facts.
2. Claim spine: write conclusion-style slide titles before drawing.
3. Design-system lock: define typography, palette, chart grammar, connector grammar, footer, and banned motifs.
4. Contact-sheet plan: vary macro-layouts and avoid three consecutive repeated compositions.
5. Editable build: prefer editable text, shapes, tables, and chart primitives.
6. Comeback rubric: score story, specificity, rhythm, whitespace, typography, precision, and coherence.
7. Mechanical verification: check PPTX package, render when possible, inspect visual defects.
8. QA ledger: record checks, tradeoffs, failures fixed, and remaining limitations.

## Rubric Minimums

For ME brand generation, use a compact 0-5 score:
- story
- specificity
- rhythm
- whitespace
- typography
- brand coherence
- precision

Target: no dimension below 4 before final delivery. If a renderer is unavailable, do not claim visual perfection.

## Anti-Patterns To Block

- Topic-label title instead of a claim.
- Repeated `title + subtitle + card grid` cadence.
- Containers louder than content.
- Same-color text on background.
- Floating arrows or connectors.
- Text clipped by filled callouts.
- Footer or page marker drift.
- Numbered circle text not centered in the circle.
- SVG icons that render as placeholder question marks.
- Fabricated official logos, partner marks, or product UI.
