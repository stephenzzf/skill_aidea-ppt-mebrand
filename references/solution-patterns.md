# Solution Deck Patterns

Use these notes for public-safe industry solution or proposal decks.

## Narrative Spine

Common structure:
1. Market / customer context.
2. Business pain and urgency.
3. Solution architecture.
4. Capability modules.
5. Delivery path and operating model.
6. Value proof or expected outcomes.
7. Roadmap / next steps.

## Page Patterns

- Problem framing: left pain cluster, right implication or opportunity.
- Capability map: 2x3 or 3x2 card grid with icons and one-line proof.
- Architecture: horizontal flow from data / channel / operation to AI / automation / growth.
- Delivery path: phased timeline with milestones and deliverables.
- Value page: metrics placeholders only when supplied by the user; otherwise use qualitative outcomes.

## Density Guidance

- Proposal pages may carry more content than executive-review pages, but avoid body text below 10pt.
- Prefer grouped bullets over paragraphs.
- Use one strong diagram per slide instead of multiple unrelated card grids.
- Do not fabricate customer logos, case metrics, official product screenshots, or partner marks.
